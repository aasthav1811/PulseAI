The Problem

Challenge: Product teams at B2B SaaS companies are drowning in customer feedback. They receive 10,000+ posts monthly across Twitter, Reddit, G2, and support tickets—but have no way to process it all efficiently.

Real Pain Points
  • Manual analysis of reviews takes 40+ hours per week
  • Teams discover brand crises days too late (after they've gone viral)
  • Can't distinguish real issues from noise at scale
  • Miss opportunities to spot competitor weaknesses
  • No visibility into which product features customers actually care about

Why This Matters: By the time a team finishes manually reviewing 1,000 posts, 5,000 more have accumulated. Crisis management becomes reactive instead of proactive. Product decisions are made without data-driven insights.

Market Gap: Existing solutions are either generic sentiment dashboards (miss context) or require data science expertise to implement (inaccessible to product teams).

The Solution

Approach: Build an automated NLP pipeline that transforms raw customer feedback into actionable intelligence—designed specifically for non-technical product managers.

Core Innovation

Instead of "here's your sentiment score," the platform answers questions product teams actually ask: "What are customers complaining about RIGHT NOW? Is this a real crisis? What features are competitors weak at?"

Key Components

  • BERT Sentiment Analysis
    RoBERTa fine-tuned on 124M tweets. Handles sarcasm, context, aspect-level sentiment ("love the UI, hate the pricing")
  • NMF Topic Modeling
    Discovers 8 recurring themes automatically. Avoids LDA's bias toward long documents—optimized for short texts like reviews
  • Multi-Signal Crisis Detection
    Weighs 10 different crisis indicators (legal threats, data breaches, outrage, viral signals). Distinguishes "customer is upset" from "company needs to activate crisis playbook"
  • Trend Forecasting
    Exponential smoothing forecasts 14-day sentiment trajectory. Anomaly detection catches inflection points before they trend
  • Competitor Intelligence
    Extracts competitor mentions, sentiment comparison, switch signals. Identifies competitive gaps to exploit

Design Decisions

Component Choice Why Not Alternative
Sentiment Model RoBERTa (Transformers) Rule-based (VADER) misses sarcasm & context. 15-20% accuracy gap on social media
Topic Modeling NMF + TF-IDF LDA assumes long documents. NMF produces 20% more coherent topics for reviews/tweets
Forecasting Exponential Smoothing ARIMA overkill for short horizon. ETS captures trend with minimal complexity
Crisis Scoring Multi-signal weighted Single sentiment score misses urgency. "Negative" can mean "slow loading" OR "company got hacked"

Error Handling & Resilience

Problem: Real-world deployment breaks. Models fail, GPU isn't available, vocab is sparse.

Fallback Strategy (3 Layers)
  • Layer 1: Transformer model (high accuracy, requires GPU/internet)
  • Layer 2: VADER lexicon (fast, offline, ~70% accuracy)
  • Layer 3: Keyword matching (guaranteed uptime, ~50% accuracy)

Same pattern for topic modeling: NMF → Keyword-based clustering. This means the dashboard is always usable, even when dependencies fail.

Real-World Scenarios

A data breach mention gets flagged immediately as 🔴 CRITICAL (multi-signal: "data breach" + "lawyer" + high engagement). Team can respond within hours, not days. Prevents social media wildfire.
✅ Scenario 2: Discovering Roadmap Priorities
